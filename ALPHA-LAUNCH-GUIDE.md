# 🚀 ALPHA LAUNCH GUIDE - CLARITY

## Overview

This guide will help you successfully launch Clarity with your first 10-15 alpha users.

**Goal:** Get real users, gather feedback, validate product-market fit

## WHO TO INVITE

### Ideal Alpha Users

**Characteristics:**
- ✅ Busy professionals with packed calendars
- ✅ People who journal or want to journal
- ✅ Interested in productivity and self-improvement
- ✅ Tech-savvy (comfortable with web apps)
- ✅ Willing to give honest feedback
- ✅ Have 15-20 min to onboard

**Where to find them:**
- Friends and colleagues
- LinkedIn connections
- Twitter/X followers
- Reddit (r/productivity, r/journaling)
- Product Hunt community
- Indie Hackers community

### Who NOT to Invite (Yet)

- ❌ People who won't use it
- ❌ Family (too polite, won't be honest)
- ❌ Non-tech users (wait for v2)
- ❌ People without busy schedules
- ❌ Critics who'll just complain

## COMMON ISSUES & FIXES

### Technical Issues

**"Can't sign up"**
→ Check Render backend is running
→ Check CORS settings
→ Check browser console

**"Calendar upload failed"**
→ Upload the .ics file from inside the ZIP, not the ZIP itself
→ File size limit is 10 MB
→ Try different browser

**"Insights not showing"**
→ Patterns need about 10 logged weekdays; the page shows progress
→ A mood must be picked on each entry
→ Check backend logs

### UX Issues

**"Don't know what to journal about"**
→ Add prompts to journal page
→ Show examples
→ Daily questions feature

**"Calendar export is confusing"**
→ Add screenshots to instructions
→ Video tutorial
→ Direct Google Calendar link

**"Too many features, overwhelming"**
→ Simplify onboarding
→ Progressive disclosure
→ Guided tour

**Priority Matrix:**

**High Impact + Easy:** Do immediately
- Quick wins
- Bug fixes
- Copy improvements

**High Impact + Hard:** Plan carefully
- Core features
- Major improvements
- Consider for v2

**Low Impact + Easy:** Maybe
- Polish items
- Small enhancements

**Low Impact + Hard:** Don't do
- Defer indefinitely
- Not worth the effort
